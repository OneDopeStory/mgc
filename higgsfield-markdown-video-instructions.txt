3. Format Specifications & Guidelines
Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts (9:16 - 1080x1920)
Duration: Exactly 10 seconds.

Hook Rule: The visual climax or core problem statement must occur within the first 1.5 seconds.

Style: High visual inertia, kinetic subtitle overlays, seamless end-to-beginning loop for maximum retention.

Interactive Web Motion (Desktop / Mobile)
Format: Responsive layout via the Higgsfield Website Skill with embedded WebM video backgrounds.

Performance: Must be lightweight, muted (muted), inline (playsinline), and looping (autoplay).

Overlay Rule: Always utilize dark glassmorphism backdrops (backdrop-blur-md bg-black/40) to maintain text legibility over motion loops.

Slide Ads & Presenter Kits (16:9 - 1920x1080)
Style: Clean negative space, structural financial or operational charts, high-density readability, and prominent CTA blocks.

5. Agentic Design Glossary
Agentic Request: An automated instruction dispatched via Slack or Google Workspace that triggers an autonomous multimedia pipeline (video generation, code scaffolding, asset assembly).

Ping-Pong Looping: A video playback mode where footage plays forward then reverses seamlessly, preventing jarring cuts on background loops.

Kinetic Typography: Text elements that animate dynamically (scaling, fading, or sliding) to match the rhythm of short-form video hooks.

Glassmorphism: A UI design style characterized by translucent frosted-glass elements, high blur levels, and delicate white borders.
