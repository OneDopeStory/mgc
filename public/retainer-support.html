Fit Check

Is a Retainer Right for You?

Retainers work best for businesses at a specific stage. Here's how to know if it's the right move.

Great Fit If You...

  • Completed a project with us and want ongoing support
  • Have automations and systems that need active monitoring
  • Don't want to hire a full-time operations or AI specialist yet
  • Need a strategic partner who understands your tech stack
  • Want to continuously improve operations month over month
  • Have a growing team that needs ongoing training and support
  • Want predictable costs for operational technology management

⬜ Not the Best Fit If You...

  • Haven't built any systems yet (start with one of our project-based services)
  • Only need a one-time project with a clear end date
  • Have an internal IT team that can manage your automation stack
  • Need full-time, 40hr/week dedicated support (we can help you hire)
  • Are looking for the cheapest option (retainers are a quality investment)
