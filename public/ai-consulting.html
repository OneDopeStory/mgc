Our Framework

The 4-Phase AI Strategy Engagement

No generic playbooks. Every engagement follows a proven structure — but the content is built entirely around your business, your data, and your goals.

1
Week 1–2

Discovery & Assessment

We don't start with what AI can do. We start with what your business actually needs. Deep-dive interviews with leadership, operations, and frontline staff to map pain points, data flows, and decision bottlenecks.

  • Stakeholder interviews (executive + operational)
  • Current tech stack inventory & data audit
  • Process mapping for high-friction workflows
  • AI Readiness Score (1–100 scale)
2
Week 2–3

Opportunity Mapping & ROI Modeling

We identify every AI opportunity in your business and rank them by impact, feasibility, and speed-to-value. Each one gets a financial model your CFO can actually evaluate.

  • Prioritized opportunity matrix (impact vs. effort)
  • ROI projections for top 5–8 initiatives
  • Tool evaluation matrix (vendor-agnostic)
  • Risk assessment for each initiative
3
Week 3–4

Strategic Roadmap Development

Not a deck that sits in a drawer. A 90-day phased plan with weekly milestones, resource assignments, and success metrics tied to your actual business KPIs.

  • 90-day phased implementation plan
  • Technology recommendation brief
  • Change management playbook
  • AI governance framework (policies, data handling, compliance)
4
Week 4+

Executive Delivery & Activation

We present findings to your leadership in their language — not ours. Then we stay to help you execute. Strategy without follow-through is just a PDF.

  • Board-ready executive briefing deck
  • Live presentation to leadership team
  • 30-day implementation kickstart support
  • Optional: Retainer for ongoing guidance
