The Difference

Why Most AI Training Fails

The gap between "we had AI training" and "our team actually uses AI" is massive. Here's why — and how we close it.

🔴 Typical AI Training

The "Now You Know" Approach

  • Generic content about ChatGPT that doesn't connect to your actual business
  • Slides and demos with no hands-on practice using real work scenarios
  • One-size-fits-all for everyone from the CEO to the receptionist
  • No follow-up — training ends and adoption is left to chance
  • Focused on what AI can do in theory, not what it should do for your team
  • Ignores governance, data privacy, and responsible use
🔵 MGC Workshop Approach

The "Now You Can" Method

  • Custom content built around your tools, workflows, and industry
  • 80% hands-on — participants build real prompts, workflows, and templates during the session
  • Role-specific tracks: leadership sees strategy, teams learn execution
  • 30-day follow-up with prompt library, resource guide, and check-in session
  • Focused on practical, day-one wins that save time immediately
  • Includes AI governance basics: what's safe, what's not, and where the lines are

Fit Check

Is This Right for You?

Our workshops deliver the most value for teams at a specific stage of AI readiness.

Great Fit If You...

  • Know your team needs AI skills but doesn't know where to start
  • Want training built around your actual tools and industry
  • Have 5–50 people who need to get up to speed
