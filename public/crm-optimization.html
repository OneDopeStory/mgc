Real-World Scenarios

CRM Optimization in Action

Different industries, same problem: a CRM that should be driving revenue but isn't. Here's how we fix it.

Home Care / Healthcare

Referral tracking scattered across 4 systems — unified into one pipeline

Referrals come from hospitals, physicians, and families through different channels. Currently tracked in email, spreadsheets, the EHR, and the CRM — with no single view of status. We build a unified referral pipeline with automated intake, status tracking, and conversion reporting.

Result: 100% referral visibility, 60% faster intake processing, zero lost referrals
Professional Services

Proposal follow-ups falling through the cracks — automated to perfection

Proposals sent, then silence. No systematic follow-up, no tracking of who opened what, no escalation when deals stall. We build automated follow-up sequences triggered by proposal status, with engagement tracking and rep notifications.

Result: 35% improvement in proposal-to-close rate, average follow-up time cut from 5 days to same-day
Real Estate

500 leads per month with no scoring — prioritized by AI

Every lead gets the same treatment: a generic email and a prayer. We implement AI-powered lead scoring based on property interest, engagement, timeline, and pre-qualification data. Hot leads route to agents instantly; nurture sequences handle the rest.

Result: Agents focus on top 20% of leads, 45% increase in conversion rate, 60% less time on unqualified leads
Staffing / Recruiting

Candidate pipeline with zero automation — transformed into a talent machine

Candidates apply, sit in a spreadsheet, get a call if someone remembers. We build a CRM pipeline that scores candidates, triggers automated status updates, schedules interviews, and alerts recruiters when top matches appear.

Result: Time-to-fill reduced by 40%, candidate experience score up 55%, recruiter capacity doubled
