Questions

Everything you need to know before you commit.

Is there a contract for membership?
No contract, no commitment. All membership tiers are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime — before your next billing date — from your client dashboard or by emailing us.
What happens after I pay for a service?
You'll receive a confirmation email and be contacted within 24 business hours to schedule your kickoff session. We move fast — most projects are scoped and underway within 48 hours.
Can I upgrade my membership tier later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade at any time and we'll prorate the difference. Most clients start at Starter or Pro and move to Elite once they see the ROI. Email us and we'll handle the upgrade immediately.
Do you offer refunds?
For project services, we offer a 7-day satisfaction window. For membership, we offer a full refund of your first month if you're not satisfied after your kickoff session. We stand behind our work.
