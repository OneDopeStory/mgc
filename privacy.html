4. AI assistants

Our booking and site assistants are AI tools. They can answer questions and book sessions, but they cannot give legal, tax, or credit advice. Please do not share sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, bank details, or health records in the chat. Chat messages are used to produce replies and may be stored with your booking request.
