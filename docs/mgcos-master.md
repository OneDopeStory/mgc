## Section 1: Executive Brief

*Scope: A 300-word orientation to MGCOS — what it is, who it serves, the three-tier model, and the three proprietary innovations that distinguish it from generic automation tooling.*

---

MGCOS (MGC Operating System) is a tiered AI operations platform built and operated by MGC Global Consulting LLC. It delivers the systems, integrations, and strategic support that service businesses need to operate on documented infrastructure rather than manual processes. MGCOS serves solo operators, growing teams, and established business owners — any operator who has identified the gap between their current manual workflows and the automated, AI-enabled business they need to build.

**The Three-Tier Model**

MGCOS is structured as three membership tiers, each calibrated to a distinct stage of operational maturity:

- **Starter ($199/mo)** — Foundation-layer access for solo operators building their first documented operational systems. Includes the MGC Resource Library, private community Slack workspace, monthly group Q&A call, up to 2 configured integrations, and up to 3 workflow templates delivered during a 7-day onboarding.

- **Pro ($499/mo)** — Execution-layer access for teams who have identified their automation opportunities and need a dedicated partner to build them. Delivers one complete custom automation workflow per month, up to 5 configured integrations, a dedicated account lead and solutions architect, and a 14-day onboarding with architecture review.

- **Elite ($1,299/mo)** — Full-engagement access for established business owners who need an always-on systems partner. Provides unlimited automation builds, weekly 60-minute 1:1 strategy sessions with Mark, same-day Slack access, a Custom AI Strategy Blueprint, and a 30-day staged onboarding with compliance scanning and role-specific team training.

**Three Proprietary Innovations**

MGCOS differentiates through three proprietary capabilities: **TrueAssist AI Chat**, a site-level AI agent available to all members and site visitors; the **Research Hub**, a dedicated research thread inside the Elite Member Dashboard; and the **MGC Knowledge Base**, an integrated reference system accessible through the Elite Member Dashboard. All tiers benefit from TrueAssist; Research Hub and Knowledge Base access are reserved for Elite members.

No contracts. All tiers are month-to-month. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated credit applied.

### 2.3 Persona Cards

#### Starter — The Solo Operator Building the Foundation

The ideal Starter client is a solo founder or early-stage operator — typically one to five people — who is profitable but still doing everything manually and has no peer network to pressure-test their AI assumptions. Their current pain is isolation compounded by information overload: they are reading everything about AI but have no structured way to decide what to implement first, and services-on-demand feel financially out of reach. After onboarding, they gain a monthly rhythm of education through the newsletter, a community of peers in the Slack workspace, a live Q&A call each month where they can surface real blockers, and a 10% discount that makes one-off engagements like the AI Readiness Assessment ($675 instead of $750) immediately cost-neutral in the first month they need it.

---

#### Pro — The Growing Team Ready to Execute

The ideal Pro client is an operator or small team lead (typically 5–25 people) who has already identified their automation opportunities but lacks the specialized bandwidth to build them — they know they're leaving time and revenue on the table every week. Their current pain is the gap between knowing and doing: they have one or two workflows eating 10+ hours per week, they've started a Zapier account and stalled, and they are the integration layer between tools that should talk to each other. After onboarding, one complete, production-ready automation workflow ships to their business every month — CRM flows, email sequences, billing triggers, or whatever returns the highest ROI that cycle — plus a monthly group strategy call with Mark and a quarterly systems review that keeps momentum from stalling between builds.

---

#### Elite — The Business Owner Who Needs a Dedicated Systems Partner

The ideal Elite client is an established business owner or operator who has moved past experimentation and needs a dedicated, always-on AI systems partner — not a consultant they brief and wait on, but someone who knows their stack, tracks their goals, and builds without requiring constant direction. Their current pain is complexity at speed: multiple simultaneous operational initiatives, a growing team that needs infrastructure before headcount, and strategic decisions that require someone who understands both the business context and the technical execution layer. After onboarding, they get weekly 60-minute 1:1s with Mark, same-day Slack response on weekdays, a custom AI Strategy Blueprint architected around their business, unlimited automation builds, and full access to the Elite Member Dashboard — a portal that centralizes projects, deliverables, invoices, the knowledge base, and a dedicated TrueAssist research thread.

### 3.5 Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q1: How long does setup take before I can actually use it?

**A:** It depends on your tier. Starter onboarding runs 7 days: your integrations are configured on Day 2, your workflow templates are delivered by Day 4, and you run your first live workflow before Day 6. Pro onboarding is 14 days and includes a deeper architecture review, up to 5 integrations, and a full workflow library of up to 8 workflows. Elite runs 30 days and covers unlimited integrations, custom workflow development, a staged production rollout, role-specific team training, and a compliance scan before go-live. Every tier starts with a Day 2 kickoff call so nothing gets built before you have confirmed what gets built first.

---

#### Q2: Is there a contract? What if I need to cancel?

**A:** No contracts. All three tiers are month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time directly from your self-serve billing portal — no email required, no notice period. If you upgrade mid-cycle, prorated credit is applied immediately. If you cancel, your access runs through the end of the period you have already paid for. The workflows, templates, and documentation delivered to your environment stay in your environment.

---

#### Q3: We don't have a technical team. Is the AI and automation complexity going to land on us to manage?

**A:** No. MGC handles the build, configuration, and validation work. Your team's role during onboarding is to complete the intake questionnaire, confirm that the integrations are flowing data correctly, and run the workflows with MGC on the call the first time. Ongoing, you use your dashboard and your support channel to flag anything that needs attention — you do not maintain the automation infrastructure yourself. The workflow templates and SOPs delivered during onboarding are written for operators, not developers.

---

#### Q4: Who owns our data? What happens to it if we cancel?

**A:** You own your data. MGCOS stores only what is necessary to run the integrations and workflows configured for your account — contact records, booking events, payment references, and workflow execution logs. None of it is used for any purpose other than operating your account. When you cancel, your account data is available for export from your portal for 30 days, after which it is deleted from MGCOS systems per our data retention schedule. Your integrations and any external systems they connect to (HubSpot, QuickBooks, Google Calendar, Notion) continue to hold whatever data was synced there — that data is governed by those platforms, not by MGCOS.

---

#### Q5: What does upgrading from one tier to the next actually look like?

**A:** Upgrades take effect immediately and prorated credit is applied to your current cycle. When you move from Starter to Pro, your account lead and solutions architect are assigned within one business day, and a transition kickoff call is scheduled to scope the first automation build and configure any additional integrations beyond what was set up at Starter. Moving from Pro to Elite follows the same pattern: your dedicated Slack channel is activated, your weekly 1:1 cadence is set up, and your Custom AI Strategy Blueprint work begins in the first 30 days of the new tier. There is no re-onboarding from scratch — your existing integrations and workflows are carried forward.

```json
{
  "event_id": "evt_01HXYZABCDEFGH",

  "timestamp": "2026-10-23T14:30:00Z",

}
```

The table below maps 12 common third-party tools to their MGCOS integration profile. **Elite Only** integrations are not available on Starter or Pro plans.

> **Connection types:**
> - **Webhook** — MGCOS pushes events to an endpoint you register
> - **OAuth 2.0** — Authorized via the user's OAuth flow in MGCOS Settings
> - **API Key** — Authenticated via a static API key stored in MGCOS Settings
> - **Native** — Built-in connector maintained by the MGCOS integration team

> **Sync directions:**
> - **Inbound** — Data flows into MGCOS
> - **Outbound** — Data flows out of MGCOS
> - **Bidirectional** — Two-way sync

| # | Tool | Connection Type | Sync Direction | Elite Only | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stripe** | OAuth 2.0 + Webhook | Bidirectional | No | Connect your Stripe account to enable payment capture, refunds, and invoice sync. MGCOS subscribes to Stripe's `charge.succeeded`, `charge.failed`, and `invoice.payment_succeeded` webhooks. Elite accounts unlock subscription billing and automatic retainer renewal. |
| 2 | **Zapier** | Webhook + API Key | Bidirectional | No | Use Zapier to connect MGCOS to 5,000+ apps. MGCOS is a Zapier trigger (fires on any webhook event) and a Zapier action (accepts calls to the MGCOS inbound API). No-code; ideal for teams without developers. |
| 3 | **Make (formerly Integromat)** | Webhook + API Key | Bidirectional | No | MGCOS native Make module available in the Make marketplace. Supports multi-step scenarios with MGCOS as both trigger and action node. Recommended over Zapier for complex multi-branch automation logic. |
| 4 | **HubSpot** | OAuth 2.0 | Bidirectional | No | Two-way sync of contacts, deals, and activity timelines. MGCOS pipeline stages map to HubSpot deal stages. Contact updates flow both directions; MGCOS is the system of record for bookings; HubSpot is the system of record for marketing engagement. |
| 5 | **Salesforce** | OAuth 2.0 | Bidirectional | **Yes** | Salesforce integration is Elite-only. Syncs MGCOS contacts to Salesforce leads/contacts, bookings to Salesforce events, and pipeline stage changes to Salesforce opportunity stages. Requires Salesforce API access (Professional edition or above). |
| 6 | **Slack** | OAuth 2.0 | Outbound | No | MGCOS posts real-time notifications to Slack channels you designate. Configurable triggers: new booking, payment received, agent escalation, review posted, pipeline stage change. Supports direct messages to assigned team members for escalations. |
| 7 | **Twilio** | API Key | Outbound | No | MGCOS uses your Twilio credentials to send SMS notifications and reminders. Used for: booking confirmations, payment receipts, and workflow SMS steps. Elite accounts unlock two-way SMS conversations managed through the MGCOS AI agent. |
| 8 | **OpenAI** | API Key | Inbound | **Yes** | Elite-only integration that allows you to supply your own OpenAI API key to power the MGCOS AI agent with your chosen GPT model. Enables custom system prompts, fine-tuned behavior, and GPT-4o for high-value client conversations. Usage is billed against your own OpenAI account. |
| 9 | **Notion** | OAuth 2.0 | Outbound | No | Exports MGCOS CRM data, meeting notes, and pipeline summaries to Notion databases you designate. Useful for building client-facing project workspaces that stay in sync with MGCOS activity. Read-back from Notion into MGCOS is not currently supported. |
| 10 | **Google Calendar** | OAuth 2.0 | Bidirectional | No | Syncs MGCOS bookings to your team's Google Calendars. New bookings create Calendar events; Calendar event cancellations reflect back in MGCOS booking status. Each team member authorizes their own calendar. Availability blocking reads from Google Calendar when checking booking slots. |
| 11 | **QuickBooks Online** | OAuth 2.0 | Outbound | **Yes** | Elite-only accounting integration. Pushes MGCOS invoices to QuickBooks as invoices, and payment events as received payments. Contacts sync as QuickBooks customers. Refunds create QuickBooks credit memos. Does not pull data from QuickBooks into MGCOS. |
| 12 | **ActiveCampaign** | API Key | Bidirectional | No | Syncs contacts and tags between MGCOS and ActiveCampaign. MGCOS pipeline stage changes trigger ActiveCampaign automations; ActiveCampaign contact field updates sync back to MGCOS custom fields. Useful for teams already running complex email automations in ActiveCampaign who want MGCOS to serve as the CRM layer. |
