## FAQ Section

*Five questions covering the most common objections before a purchase decision.*

---

### Q1: How long does setup take before I can actually use it?

**A:** It depends on your tier. Starter onboarding runs 7 days: your integrations are configured on Day 2, your workflow templates are delivered by Day 4, and you run your first live workflow before Day 6. Pro onboarding is 14 days and includes a deeper architecture review, up to 5 integrations, and a full workflow library of up to 8 workflows. Elite runs 30 days and covers unlimited integrations, custom workflow development, a staged production rollout, role-specific team training, and a compliance scan before go-live. Every tier starts with a Day 2 kickoff call so nothing gets built before you have confirmed what gets built first.

---

### Q2: Is there a contract? What if I need to cancel?

**A:** No contracts. All three tiers are month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time directly from your self-serve billing portal — no email required, no notice period. If you upgrade mid-cycle, prorated credit is applied immediately. If you cancel, your access runs through the end of the period you have already paid for. The workflows, templates, and documentation delivered to your environment stay in your environment.

---

### Q3: We don't have a technical team. Is the AI and automation complexity going to land on us to manage?

**A:** No. MGC handles the build, configuration, and validation work. Your team's role during onboarding is to complete the intake questionnaire, confirm that the integrations are flowing data correctly, and run the workflows with MGC on the call the first time. Ongoing, you use your dashboard and your support channel to flag anything that needs attention — you do not maintain the automation infrastructure yourself. The workflow templates and SOPs delivered during onboarding are written for operators, not developers.

---

### Q4: Who owns our data? What happens to it if we cancel?

**A:** You own your data. MGCOS stores only what is necessary to run the integrations and workflows configured for your account — contact records, booking events, payment references, and workflow execution logs. None of it is used for any purpose other than operating your account. When you cancel, your account data is available for export from your portal for 30 days, after which it is deleted from MGCOS systems per our data retention schedule. Your integrations and any external systems they connect to (HubSpot, QuickBooks, Google Calendar, Notion) continue to hold whatever data was synced there — that data is governed by those platforms, not by MGCOS.

---

### Q5: What does upgrading from one tier to the next actually look like?

**A:** Upgrades take effect immediately and prorated credit is applied to your current cycle. When you move from Starter to Pro, your account lead and solutions architect are assigned within one business day, and a transition kickoff call is scheduled to scope the first automation build and configure any additional integrations beyond what was set up at Starter. Moving from Pro to Elite follows the same pattern: your dedicated Slack channel is activated, your weekly 1:1 cadence is set up, and your Custom AI Strategy Blueprint work begins in the first 30 days of the new tier. There is no re-onboarding from scratch — your existing integrations and workflows are carried forward.
