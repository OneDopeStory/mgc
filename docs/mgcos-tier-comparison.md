## Artifact 2 — Persona Cards

### Starter — The Solo Operator Building the Foundation

The ideal Starter client is a solo founder or early-stage operator — typically one to five people — who is profitable but still doing everything manually and has no peer network to pressure-test their AI assumptions. Their current pain is isolation compounded by information overload: they are reading everything about AI but have no structured way to decide what to implement first, and services-on-demand feel financially out of reach. After onboarding, they gain a monthly rhythm of education through the newsletter, a community of peers in the Slack workspace, a live Q&A call each month where they can surface real blockers, and a 10% discount that makes one-off engagements like the AI Readiness Assessment ($675 instead of $750) immediately cost-neutral in the first month they need it.

---

### Pro — The Growing Team Ready to Execute

The ideal Pro client is an operator or small team lead (typically 5–25 people) who has already identified their automation opportunities but lacks the specialized bandwidth to build them — they know they're leaving time and revenue on the table every week. Their current pain is the gap between knowing and doing: they have one or two workflows eating 10+ hours per week, they've started a Zapier account and stalled, and they are the integration layer between tools that should talk to each other. After onboarding, one complete, production-ready automation workflow ships to their business every month — CRM flows, email sequences, billing triggers, or whatever returns the highest ROI that cycle — plus a monthly group strategy call with Mark and a quarterly systems review that keeps momentum from stalling between builds.

---

### Elite — The Business Owner Who Needs a Dedicated Systems Partner

The ideal Elite client is an established business owner or operator who has moved past experimentation and needs a dedicated, always-on AI systems partner — not a consultant they brief and wait on, but someone who knows their stack, tracks their goals, and builds without requiring constant direction. Their current pain is complexity at speed: multiple simultaneous operational initiatives, a growing team that needs infrastructure before headcount, and strategic decisions that require someone who understands both the business context and the technical execution layer. After onboarding, they get weekly 60-minute 1:1s with Mark, same-day Slack response on weekdays, a custom AI Strategy Blueprint architected around their business, unlimited automation builds, and full access to the Elite Member Dashboard — a portal that centralizes projects, deliverables, invoices, the knowledge base, and a dedicated TrueAssist research thread.

## Artifact 3 — Upgrade Trigger Table

### Starter → Pro

The following signals indicate a Starter member is ready to move to Pro. Each signal is derived from the capability gap between the tiers.

| # | Business Signal |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Recurring manual bottleneck identified.** You leave the monthly Q&A call knowing exactly which workflow is costing you 5–10 hours per week and no one on your team has bandwidth to build it. |
| 2 | **Stalled DIY automation attempt.** You started a Zapier or Make.com build, hit a decision point, and it sat unfinished for more than two weeks — the cost of inaction is now visible. |
| 3 | **Service discount value exceeds the tier delta.** You are planning a one-off engagement (e.g., Workflow Audit at its standard rate) where a 15% vs. 10% discount would save more than the $300/mo difference between Starter and Pro. |
| 4 | **Team growth requires operational documentation.** You are onboarding your first employee or contractor and realize you have no documented SOPs or automated onboarding sequences to hand them. |
| 5 | **You've outgrown Q&A-only strategy.** The monthly group Q&A is no longer enough — you need a structured monthly strategy call with Mark where your specific priorities are on the agenda, not just reactive questions. |

---

### Pro → Elite

The following signals indicate a Pro member is ready to move to Elite. Each signal is derived from the capability gap between the tiers.

| # | Business Signal |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Build backlog exceeds 1 per month.** You consistently identify two or more high-ROI automations per month but the single-build limit means you are always choosing between them — compounding delay is now measurable in revenue. |
| 2 | **Strategic direction is the bottleneck, not execution.** You are no longer sure which systems to build next — you need a dedicated advisor who knows your stack and goals, not just someone to fulfill a build request once a month. |
| 3 | **Major operational overhaul is on the roadmap.** You are planning a full CRM build, an end-to-end lead-to-invoice automation, or a reporting infrastructure project that requires sustained, multi-month engagement beyond one build per cycle. |
| 4 | **Scaling headcount requires infrastructure to exist first.** You are preparing to hire two or more people and need AI-enabled processes, onboarding automations, and documented systems in place before their start dates — not after. |
| 5 | **Quarterly review cadence is too slow.** Your business is evolving faster than quarterly — competitive shifts, new revenue lines, or a funding event mean you need weekly strategic guidance with someone who can translate business context into system architecture in real time. |
