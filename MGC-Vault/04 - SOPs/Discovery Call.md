# Discovery Call SOP

## Purpose
Qualify the lead, understand their situation deeply, and position MGC as the only logical choice. Target: 45 minutes.

## Trigger
Lead books a call from the website, referral, or outreach response.

---

## Call Structure

### Opening (5 min)
- Thank them for their time, set agenda
- "I have about 45 minutes carved out for you — I want to spend most of it understanding your situation. Sound good?"

### Their World (15 min) — LISTEN MORE THAN TALK
1. "Tell me about your business — what do you do and who do you serve?"
2. "Walk me through a typical week operationally — what does your team spend most time on?"
3. "Where does your business feel like it's running through mud right now?"

### The Problem (10 min) — DIG DEEP
4. "How long has that been an issue?"
5. "What have you tried to solve it?"
6. "What's it costing you — time, money, team frustration?"
7. "If you could fix just one thing in the next 90 days, what would it be?"

### The Vision (5 min)
8. "What does success look like for you a year from now?"
9. "What's the number — revenue, clients, team size?"

### Qualification (5 min)
10. "What's your timeline for making a decision on this?"
11. "Is there anyone else involved in this decision?"
12. "Do you have a budget range in mind?"

### Close / Next Step (5 min)
- "Based on what you've shared, I think we can help. Here's what I'd recommend..."
- Propose a specific engagement
- "Does that make sense as a starting point?"
- Set a follow-up date on the call

## Signals to Watch
| Green Light | Red Light |
|---|---|
| Specific problem, clear pain | Vague answers, can't articulate problem |
| Decision maker on the call | "I need to run it by someone" without them present |
| Timeline in 30–90 days | "Someday" or "just exploring" |
| Budget aligned | Fishing for lowest price |
| Engaged, asks questions | Distracted, short answers |
