# AI Strategy Playbook

## Service Overview
Custom AI roadmaps, LLM integrations, and AI readiness assessments for growth-stage businesses.

## Ideal Client Profile
- Revenue: $500K–$10M
- Team size: 5–50
- Pain: Manual processes, data silos, slow decision-making
- Goal: Reduce operational overhead, improve margins

---

## Discovery Questions
1. What are your highest-volume repetitive tasks?
2. Where does information get stuck or lost in your business?
3. What would you do with 20 extra hours per week?
4. What data do you collect but never use?
5. What's the cost of your current manual processes?

---

## Engagement Phases

### Phase 1 — AI Readiness Assessment (Week 1–2)
- Audit current tools and workflows
- Map data sources and quality
- Identify top 3 automation opportunities
- Score readiness (infrastructure, team, data)
- Deliver: Readiness Report + Prioritized Roadmap

### Phase 2 — Pilot Implementation (Week 3–6)
- Select highest-ROI use case
- Build and test AI solution
- Train team on usage
- Measure baseline vs. results
- Deliver: Working AI tool + training documentation

### Phase 3 — Scale & Systematize (Week 7–12)
- Expand to second and third use cases
- Build internal AI governance framework
- Establish monitoring and iteration cadence
- Deliver: Full AI stack + ongoing support plan
